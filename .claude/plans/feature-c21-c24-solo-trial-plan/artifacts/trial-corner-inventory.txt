THE THREE THINGS THIS PROVES

1. The exclusion works. 14 TaskPoint-tagged parts exist; 2 carry TrialOnly=true; the round's draw pool
   is exactly 12, which is Config.Tasks.PoolSize. Before TaskService.discoverPool learned to skip the
   attribute this would have read 14, and a round could have drawn an objective into this corner.

2. The corner is isolated. Nearest ROUND task point is Task_RingCistern at 419 studs. The four walls
   are present at the floor's edges (floor spans z 395..535, x -535..-395; walls sit on those lines),
   so a player teleported in cannot walk out into empty space.

3. The contract parts are tagged as TrialService expects: TrialSpawn x1, TrialChase x1, and two
   TaskPoint parts attributed TrialOnly.
