Every tick moves the rig EXACTLY 6.5 studs (= ChaseWalkSpeed * SnapshotInterval = 13 * 0.5). This
confirms (b) the rig visibly changes position every tick under PivotTo — it does move — but each step is
larger than the entire ChaseStopStuds=6 radius, so the stop check (which only runs BEFORE each step) can
be skipped over in one tick.

Continuing to poll (same run, 14 more ticks after the above): the rig closed in and then held completely
steady, tick after tick, at:

  rig = (-509.8, 0.4, 465.0),  player = (-511.5, 3.5, 465.0),  horizontal distance = 1.70 studs

...for 14 consecutive ticks (≈8.4 seconds) with zero movement — the "hold and face the player" branch,
correctly entered once inside ChaseStopStuds, but ChaseStopStuds is documented and intended to mean the
rig stops ~6 studs away. It actually stopped at 1.70 studs. This is deterministic given the fixed
TrialChase_Far / TrialSpawn_Entry anchors and Config numbers: 86.2 mod 6.5 = 1.70, so it will
land there every time under the current Config, not just this once.

═══ RUN B — the salt/stun test ═══

Control confirmed the rig moves 6.5 studs/tick unattended. This run tests whether carrying the pouch to
the rig actually stuns it, tested as early as possible (right as the rig spawns, far from the player, so
any halt cannot be explained by the ChaseStopStuds branch):

rig-just-appeared    | pouch=(-511.5, 3.5, 461.0) [still near player, untouched]
                      | rig=(-418.8, 0.4, 465.0)  [≈93 studs from player]
pouch-dropped-on-rig | pouch manually moved to rig.CFrame * CFrame.new(2,0,0) — 2 studs from the rig,
                      | inside Config.Salt.PickupRangeStuds (6)
post-drop-tick-1     | pouch = nil (destroyed)                | rig = (-418.8, 0.4, 465.0)  [unchanged]
post-drop-tick-2     | pouch = nil                             | rig = (-418.8, 0.4, 465.0)  [unchanged]
post-drop-tick-3     | pouch = nil                             | rig = (-418.8, 0.4, 465.0)  [unchanged]
post-drop-tick-4     | pouch = nil                             | rig = (-418.8, 0.4, 465.0)  [unchanged]

Over 2.4 seconds (4 ticks) after the pouch reached PickupRangeStuds of the rig:
  - the pouch was destroyed within one tick (the proximity check fired correctly)
  - the rig did not move AT ALL, despite being ≈93 studs from the player — far outside ChaseStopStuds,
    where it would otherwise have advanced ≈31 studs (4 × 6.5) toward the player in that time.

CONCLUSION: carrying the pouch into the rig DOES produce a real stun that halts the chase, independent of
and prior to the ChaseStopStuds mechanic. This is a genuine, working positive finding — the trial's core
"salt stops the aswang" lesson is mechanically true, even though the THROW itself is not taught (known,
already-documented gap).
