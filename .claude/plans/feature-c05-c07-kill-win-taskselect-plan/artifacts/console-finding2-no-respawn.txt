FINDING 2 (exploit-auditor, High) — reproduced live, 2026-08-11. CONFIRMED, with a corrected mechanism.

THE CLAIM
  RoundService.Start sets `Players.CharacterAutoLoads = false`, and applyBodyRule is called from only
  three sites (onPlayerAdded, setAllPlayerStates, MarkKilled). None is a death handler. So a death that
  is NOT a kill — Escape > Reset Character, or falling out of the map — never respawns the player.

WHAT I REPRODUCED (server datamodel, `humanoid.Health = 0`, which is what Reset does)

  [F2] CharacterAutoLoads = false
  [F2] before: health=100 parent=Workspace
  [F2] set Health = 0; Died should fire now
  [F2] t+ 2s  Character=Demiurgos_18  sameInstance=true  health=0
  ...  (unchanged every 2s) ...
  [F2] t+16s  Character=Demiurgos_18  sameInstance=true  health=0

  Then across a FULL round boundary, 64 seconds, spanning ENDING -> INTERMISSION -> STARTING -> ACTIVE
  twice over:

  [F2] t+  4s  same=true  health=0  state=Enum.HumanoidStateType.Dead
  ...  (identical every 4s) ...
  [F2] t+ 64s  same=true  health=0  state=Enum.HumanoidStateType.Dead

  RespawnTime is 3s. The player never recovers — not after 5x RespawnTime, not at a phase transition,
  not at a new round. They are dead for the remainder of the server session.

AND THEY ARE STILL COUNTED ALIVE — this is the damaging half. Every client snapshot during rounds 2
and 3, while the character lay dead:

  [Client] Snapshot — STARTING round #2 · ... · alive 1 · you: ALIVE (3s left)
  [Client] Snapshot — ACTIVE   round #2 · ... · alive 1 · you: ALIVE (44s left)
  [Client] Snapshot — STARTING round #3 · ... · alive 1 · you: ALIVE (3s left)
  [Client] Snapshot — ACTIVE   round #3 · ... · alive 1 · you: ALIVE (44s left)

  RoundService never learns about a death it did not cause, so PlayerStates still says ALIVE,
  livingSurvivorCount still counts them, and C06's attrition win can never reach its threshold.

WHERE THE AUDITOR'S MECHANISM IS WRONG, AND WHY IT MATTERS
  The finding says `player.Character` becomes nil, so validateAndKill bails at its
  `targetCharacter == nil` guard and the player becomes UNKILLABLE. That is not what happens.

  Roblox leaves the dead character model attached: Character is non-nil, parented to Workspace, with a
  HumanoidRootPart present. Measured above. So the early return does NOT fire, KillValidation DOES run,
  TargetState is still ALIVE, and the player remains a valid kill target.

  This makes the bug LESS severe than reported — the Aswang can still kill a reset player, so the round
  is not permanently unwinnable — but it does not remove it. A player who resets is a permanently prone
  corpse who still counts as a living survivor, and the Aswang must walk over and kill a body to
  progress the win condition.

ONE READING TO DISREGARD. An earlier probe in this session showed `health=100 state=Running` after a
kill, which looked like a recovery. It was not: the console shows `[RoundService] Server closing` and a
fresh `[Bootstrap] Ready. 13 services loaded.` between the two probes — Studio had restarted the
session. The controlled test above holds the same character instance throughout and shows no recovery.

THE FIX (not applied — this is a report, not a change)
  RoundService must own respawn as well as spawn: connect `Humanoid.Died` / `CharacterRemoving` and
  re-run applyBodyRule, with the kill path excluded because makeCorpse detaches rather than kills. A
  bodiless-or-dead ALIVE player should also not be counted by livingSurvivorCount.
