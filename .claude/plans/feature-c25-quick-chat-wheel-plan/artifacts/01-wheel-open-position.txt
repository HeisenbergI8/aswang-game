execute_luau (Client datamodel), immediately after keyDown(B):

Query:
  PlayerGui children: Freecam (Enabled=true), Hud (Enabled=true), QuickChatWheel (Enabled=true), RoleIntro (Enabled=true)
  QuickChatWheel Enabled=true framePos={0, 0}, {0, 0}

Interpretation: the wheel opened (Enabled=true), but its Frame.Position is {0,0},{0,0} -- the
top-left corner of the screen, not the press point. Screenshot 01-wheel-open-via-B-keyboard.png
(saved separately by the tool) shows sector buttons clipped against the top-left corner, confirming
this is not a coordinate-reporting artifact.

Root cause (read from source, src/client/Controllers/QuickChatController.luau lines ~419-429):
  onWheelAction(_actionName, inputState, inputObject)
    local position = Vector2.new(inputObject.Position.X, inputObject.Position.Y)
    if inputState == Enum.UserInputState.Begin then
      openAt(position)   -- uses inputObject.Position

For a KEYBOARD input (Enum.KeyCode.B pressed as a key, not a touch or mouse button),
InputObject.Position is not meaningful and Roblox reports it as (0, 0, 0). openAt() then sets
frame.Position = UDim2.fromOffset(0, 0) -- the corner, not the cursor.

The header comment ("THE WHEEL OPENS AT THE THUMB, NOT AT SCREEN CENTRE") is true only for a TOUCH
press, where InputObject.Position is the real touch coordinate. For the PC keyboard bind (B), which
is the primary way a mouse+keyboard player triggers this, the position is always (0,0) regardless of
where the mouse cursor is.
