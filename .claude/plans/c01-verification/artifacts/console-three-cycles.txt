WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES
  1. Phase order   IDLE -> INTERMISSION -> STARTING -> ACTIVE -> ENDING -> INTERMISSION
                   Three consecutive cycles, no skipped phase, no repeated phase.
  2. RoundNumber   increments exactly ONCE per cycle, at STARTING: #1, #2, #3.
                   Not once per phase, which was the failure mode being checked.
  3. Snapshot      RoundSnapshot reaches the client throughout; AlivePlayerCount goes
                   0 -> 1 at STARTING and back to 0 at the following INTERMISSION.
  4. PlayerState   YourState reads ALIVE for the whole round and LOBBY between rounds.
                   This field is populated server-side by RoundService.GetPlayerState(),
                   so it is the reliable channel for that call. (A direct execute_luau
                   probe is NOT: datamodel_type="Server" runs with its own module
                   require-cache and returns a fresh un-Init()'d RoundService reading
                   IDLE/LOBBY while the live service is in ACTIVE.)

NOT COVERED HERE
  - Mid-round join -> SPECTATOR        (needs a second client joining during ACTIVE)
  - Leave during ACTIVE -> round finishes rather than ending early
    The transition table itself is proven exhaustively for both by
    tests/round-transitions.test.luau; what is untested is the Roblox wiring around it.
