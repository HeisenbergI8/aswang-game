UIController.Start()'s PhaseChanged handler resets `lastRatio = -1` (and lastCompleted,
lastGateOpen) as soon as the phase becomes anything other than Active, which fires on the
ACTIVE->ENDING transition, i.e. before the new round's first snapshot ever arrives. Because
`lastRatio` is negative when the next snapshot's ratio is computed, render() takes the
direct-assign branch (`if lastRatio < 0 then fill.Size = ... `) rather than tweenTo, so the
reset itself is a silent snap-to-empty during Intermission/Starting, off-screen before the
player is even looking at an active bar again — structurally prevents the "drain from full"
replay the question is checking for. I was not able to force a >0-task round to end in the

capture the positive case directly on the live Fill instance the way Q1-Q3 were captured.
